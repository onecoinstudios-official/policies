Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Bento Rush mobile game (“the Game,” developed and published by One Coin Studios, “we”) handles.

The short version: the Game collects nothing and shares nothing.

What the Game stores

Your scores and statistics (best score, longest streak, biggest cascade, tiles cleared, per-ingredient totals, accuracy) are stored on your device only, using the device’s standard local key-value storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device;
  • is not backed up to our servers (we have none);
  • disappears if you uninstall the Game or clear its storage from your operating system’s app settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The Game contains no analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, crash-reporting services, account systems, or network communication. The Game does not require any Android permissions.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, of any age — because it does not collect information at all. There are no chat features, no user-generated content, and no external links inside the Game.

Third-party services

None. (Distribution of the Game is via Google Play, whose own policies apply separately to the platform.)

Your choices

  • Toggle sound from the in-game speaker button.
  • Delete all locally stored records by uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage in system settings.

Changes

If a future version of the Game ever adds, changes or removes data handling, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before that version ships.
